The Method

How micro-suction works

Four precise stages, all visible to you on the treatment screen.

1

Microscopic Examination

A high-definition otoscope camera shows us — and you — the exact position, type and depth of the wax before we begin.

3–5 min
2

Gentle Suction

A fine sterile tip delivers medical-grade suction at carefully controlled pressure, lifting wax away without touching the canal walls.

10–15 min
3

Micro-Instrument Finishing

For wax adhered to the canal wall, we use micro-instruments under direct vision — never blind scraping.

5 min
4

Verification Scan

A final camera pass confirms the canal is completely clear and healthy. You see the result yourself before we finish.

2–3 min

Is It Right For You?

Suitable for almost everyone

Micro-suction is the only wax removal method recommended for people with perforated eardrums, grommets, ear infections or previous ear surgery — because it uses no water at all.

  • Perforated eardrums & grommets
  • Previous ear surgery
  • Hearing aid users
  • Sensitive or anxious clients
  • Children over 5 (with consent)
