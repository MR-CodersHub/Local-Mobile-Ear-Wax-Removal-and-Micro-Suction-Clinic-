Privacy & Data

Your privacy, taken seriously

How we collect, use, store and protect your personal and health information.

Last updated: October 2026
Privacy Policy

How we handle your information

Auris Care Ltd (“we”, “us”) provides ear wax removal, micro-suction and hearing health services from 24 Harley Street, Marylebone, London W1G 9PY and through mobile home visits. This policy explains what information we collect, why we need it, and the rights you have under UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to provide safe, effective care and to run our service:

  • Contact details — name, phone number, email address, postcode and address for home visits
  • Health information — ear symptoms, medical history, medications, previous treatments and examination findings
  • Appointment records — bookings, attendance, treatment notes, hearing reports and aftercare plans
  • Account details — login credentials and preferences for your patient portal account
  • Payment information — billing details and invoices, processed securely (we never store full card numbers)
  • Technical data — basic website usage information that helps us keep the site secure and working well

2. How we use your information

Your information is used strictly for the purposes of your care and our service:

  • Arranging, confirming and reminding you about appointments
  • Providing clinical assessment, treatment and aftercare
  • Maintaining accurate clinical records as required of registered practitioners
  • Processing payments and managing invoices
  • Responding to enquiries, feedback and complaints
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations, including CQC requirements

We never sell your information, and we never use your health information for marketing without your explicit consent.

3. Our legal bases

Under UK GDPR we process your information on these lawful bases: consent (for example, optional marketing emails you subscribe to), contract (providing the appointments and treatments you book), legal obligation (clinical record-keeping and regulatory compliance), and legitimate interests (running a safe, secure service, such as fraud prevention). Health information is processed by registered health professionals under obligations of confidentiality.

4. Sharing your information

We share information only where necessary: with members of your care team at Auris Care; with trusted service providers who help us operate (such as secure booking, payment and IT providers, bound by strict contracts); with your GP or other clinicians where you ask us to or where clinically important; and with regulators or authorities where the law requires it. We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep it

Clinical records are retained in line with UK professional guidance — generally a minimum of eight years after treatment for adults (longer for children) — after which they are securely destroyed. Account and enquiry records are kept only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected, and marketing consents until you withdraw them.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete information where there is no lawful reason to keep it
  • Restrict or object to certain uses of your information
  • Receive your information in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is our basis
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you remain unhappy

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We respond to all requests within one month.

7. Cookies on this website

This website uses a small number of cookies and local storage purely to make the site work — remembering your theme (light/dark) and layout direction (LTR/RTL) preferences. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings without affecting your care.

8. Security

We protect your information with appropriate technical and organisational measures: secure systems with access limited to authorised staff, encrypted connections on our website and patient portal, and confidentiality obligations for every member of our clinical team. No method is perfectly secure, but we review our protections regularly.

Questions about your privacy?

Contact our privacy team at hello@auriscare.co.uk, call 020 7123 4567, or write to Auris Care Ltd, 24 Harley Street, Marylebone, London W1G 9PY. If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, where the changes are significant, let you know directly.
