The Treatment Journey

Exactly what happens, at every stage

No surprises, no mystery. Here is the complete journey from booking to aftercare — so you know precisely what to expect.

The Complete Journey

Six stages, total clarity

1

Booking & Confirmation

Book online in two minutes or by phone. You receive instant confirmation by email and SMS, plus a reminder 24 hours before. Rescheduling is free up to 24 hours ahead.

Before you arrive
2

Arrival & Settling In

Arrive to a calm, scent-free treatment suite. Your specialist greets you by name, offers a drink, and walks you through exactly what will happen — you set the pace.

5 min
3

Consultation & History

We review your hearing concerns, medical history, medications, previous ear procedures and any symptoms like tinnitus, dizziness or pain. This shapes everything that follows.

5–10 min
4

Microscopic Examination

Using a high-definition otoscope camera, we examine both ears and show you the live image on screen. You will see exactly what is causing your symptoms before any treatment begins.

3–5 min
5

The Treatment

Gentle micro-suction (or irrigation where appropriate) removes the wax while you watch. You can ask us to pause at any moment. Most treatments are complete in 10–20 minutes.

10–20 min
6

Verification & Aftercare

A final camera scan confirms the canal is clear and healthy. You receive written aftercare guidance, a treatment summary, and direct contact details for your specialist should any questions arise.

5 min

During Treatment

What you'll feel and hear

Honesty matters, so here is exactly what the treatment feels like — no sugar-coating.

  • A gentle pressure and soft popping or suction sounds
  • Occasional mild tickling as wax lifts away
  • No water, no mess, no pain for the vast majority
  • The odd satisfying moment — many clients say it is oddly relaxing
  • A sudden, wonderful clarity as sound returns
Feeling anxious?

Tell us before we begin. We can go slower, pause whenever you need, and a numbing spray is available for sensitive ears. You are always in control.

Prepare Perfectly

How to prepare in the days before

Days 1–5 Before

If advised in your booking confirmation, apply olive oil ear drops twice daily. This softens wax for gentler, faster removal. Never use drops if you have a perforated eardrum unless we have told you to.

Always Avoid

Cotton buds, ear candles, hairpins and any home "removal" tools. They compact wax deeper and risk perforating the eardrum. Ear candles are proven ineffective and carry burn risks.

Bring With You

A list of medications, details of any ear surgery or perforations, and your hearing aids if you wear them. Arrive five minutes early to settle in — there is never any rush.

After Your Treatment

Aftercare that protects your results

Keep ears dry — no swimming, and shield them with a shower cap or cotton wool coated in petroleum jelly while bathing. Avoid flying if you have had significant wax removal, and do not insert anything into the ear.
Mild popping, slight sensitivity or a feeling of "openness" for 24–48 hours is completely normal. Some clients notice their own voice sounds different for a day — this settles quickly.
Contact us the same day for pain, bleeding, discharge, sudden hearing change or dizziness. These are rare, but we would always rather hear from you early. Your specialist's direct line is in your aftercare pack.
Never use cotton buds. If you are prone to wax, use olive oil drops once a fortnight as maintenance, and book a professional check every 6–12 months. Hearing aid users should have domes and tubing checked regularly.
