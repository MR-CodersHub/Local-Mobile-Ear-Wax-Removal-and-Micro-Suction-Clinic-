Our Story

Built on a simple belief: ear care should be gentle

Auris Care began in 2008 when our founder, an NHS audiologist, grew tired of watching patients endure rushed, uncomfortable ear syringing. She imagined something different — a clinic where precision met calm.

  • HCPC, GMC and NMC-registered clinicians only
  • Micro-suction trained to BSA and ENT society standards
  • Fully insured mobile service covering 40 postcodes
  • Transparent pricing — the price you see is the price you pay

The Journey

What to expect, step by step

1

Arrival & Comfort

Settle into the treatment chair with a warm drink. We review your history, medications and any previous ear procedures — nothing is rushed.

5 min
2

Microscopic Examination

Using a high-definition otoscope camera, we show you exactly what is happening inside your ear on screen before touching anything.

5 min
3

The Treatment

Gentle micro-suction or irrigation while you watch. Most clients are surprised by how quick and comfortable it is — many say it is oddly satisfying.

15–20 min
4

Verification & Aftercare

We re-scan to confirm the canal is perfectly clear, then send you home with written aftercare and direct access to your specialist.

5 min
