Our Signature Treatment

Why micro-suction is the safest way to clear wax

Unlike old-fashioned syringing, micro-suction never introduces water into the ear. A fine, sterile tip and medical-grade suction work under direct microscopic vision — meaning we see exactly what we are doing, at all times.

  • No water — safe with perforated eardrums, grommets and ear infections
  • Microscopic vision for millimetre precision
  • Single-use sterile tips for every client
  • Instant results — most clients hear the difference immediately

Before & After

Preparation and aftercare

Before your appointment

  • Use olive oil ear drops for 3–5 days beforehand if advised (softens wax for easier removal)
  • Avoid cotton buds, ear candles and inserting anything into the ear
  • Tell us about any ear surgery, perforations, infections or hearing aids
  • Eat normally and stay hydrated — no fasting required

After your treatment

  • Keep ears dry for 48 hours — no swimming or submerging
  • Avoid cotton buds; let the ear clean itself naturally
  • Mild popping or slight sensitivity for a day is normal
  • Contact us immediately for pain, discharge or sudden hearing change
