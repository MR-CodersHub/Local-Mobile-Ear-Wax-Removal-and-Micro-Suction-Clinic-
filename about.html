About Auris Care

The people behind the calm

Eighteen years of ear health excellence — built on registration, rigour and genuine human warmth.

Our Story

From one frustrated audiologist to London's most trusted ear care clinic

In 2008, Dr. Elena Marsh watched a nervous patient flinch through yet another rushed ear syringing at an overstretched GP practice. She knew ear care could be better — quieter, kinder, more precise.

So she built it. A clinic where every appointment is unhurried, every instrument is sterile, and every client leaves understanding exactly what happened to their ears and why.

Today, Auris Care is a team of four registered specialists treating over 12,000 clients a year — in our Marylebone clinic and in living rooms across 40 postcodes.

Patient-firstsince 2008
What We Stand For

Four values, every single visit

Clinical Rigour

Registered practitioners, evidence-based methods and protocols audited to CQC standards. No shortcuts, ever.

Gentle Humanity

We treat people, not ear canals. Nerves are normal here — we move at your pace and explain everything first.

Radical Transparency

You see what we see on the otoscope camera, you know the price before you book, and you get a written report after.

Immaculate Standards

Single-use sterile tips for every client, hospital-grade disinfection between appointments, full PPE as standard.

Time Respect

Appointments start on time and never feel rushed. Thirty unhurried minutes beats a ten-minute conveyor belt.

Care Without Barriers

Mobile units, evening slots and honest pricing exist because great ear care should be reachable by everyone.
