Fair Terms

Fair terms, plain language

What you can expect from us — and what we ask of you — when you book and receive care.

Last updated: October 2026
Terms & Conditions

Our agreement with you

These terms apply whenever you book or receive services from Auris Care Ltd (“we”, “us”), at our clinic at 24 Harley Street, Marylebone, London W1G 9PY or through our mobile home-visit service. By booking an appointment, you agree to these terms.

1. Our services

We provide ear wax removal (micro-suction and irrigation), hearing health checks, tinnitus assessments and related ear care, delivered by HCPC, GMC or NMC-registered clinicians. Every appointment begins with an examination and consultation; treatment only proceeds where your clinician considers it safe and appropriate for you.

2. Who can book

  • Appointments are for adults aged 18 and over, or for children accompanied by a parent or legal guardian
  • Please tell us about relevant medical conditions, medications, previous ear surgery, perforations, infections or discharge when booking
  • If you have severe ear pain, sudden hearing loss, dizziness, discharge or bleeding, seek urgent medical advice first — we are not an emergency service

3. Appointments & bookings

You can book online, by phone on 020 7123 4567, or by email at hello@auriscare.co.uk. Your booking is confirmed once you receive our confirmation message. Please arrive a few minutes early; if you arrive very late we may need to shorten or reschedule your appointment so the next client is not kept waiting.

4. Fees & payment

  • The price shown on our pricing page is the price you pay — consultation, treatment, aftercare and reports are always included
  • Mobile home visits carry an additional call-out fee, shown clearly before you confirm
  • Payment is due at the time of treatment unless we have agreed otherwise in writing
  • Invoices and receipts are available in your patient portal at any time

5. Cancellations & rescheduling

Life happens — rescheduling is free when you give us at least 24 hours’ notice, and you can do it from your patient portal or by contacting us. Appointments cancelled or missed with less than 24 hours’ notice may be subject to a cancellation fee of up to the full treatment price, at our reasonable discretion.

6. Suitability & consent

Your clinician will explain the findings, the proposed treatment, and any material risks before proceeding, and treatment only goes ahead with your informed consent — which you may withdraw at any time, including mid-treatment. Occasionally the safest advice is not to treat (for example, with active infection or a suspected perforation); where that happens we will explain why, advise on next steps, and you will only be charged for the consultation.

7. Home visits

For mobile visits, please provide accurate address and access details, a parking option where possible, and a clean, well-lit space with a chair for treatment. Our clinicians carry the right to decline or stop a visit if the environment is unsafe, in which case our cancellation terms apply.

Not an emergency service

We offer planned, non-urgent ear care. For sudden hearing loss, severe pain, head injury, heavy bleeding or signs of serious infection, contact your GP urgently, call NHS 111, or attend A&E.

8. Liability

We provide our services with reasonable skill and care, in line with professional clinical standards. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, fraud, or any liability that cannot lawfully be limited. To the extent permitted by law, our total liability for any appointment is limited to the fee paid for that appointment.

9. Complaints

If anything falls short, please tell us first — most concerns are resolved quickly with a conversation. Contact us at hello@auriscare.co.uk or 020 7123 4567 and we will acknowledge your complaint within three working days and aim to resolve it within twenty. These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

Your data & these terms

How we handle your personal and health information is set out in our Privacy Policy, which forms part of your agreement with us. We may update these terms from time to time; the version in force at the time of your booking applies.
